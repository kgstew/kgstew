I started woodworking with my dad in high school and kept going. Welding came much later, in San
Francisco, and mostly changed what was possible rather than what I wanted to make.

Recent work is furniture in welded steel and slab wood with lighting and sound built into it. This
is a capability more than a practice — it is the reason I can talk to a fabricator about a piece
rather than only about a drawing.
