I started woodworking with my dad in high school and kept going. Welding came much later, in San
Francisco, and mostly changed what was possible rather than what I wanted to make.

Recent work is furniture in welded steel and slab wood. Only the bar has electronics in it.

None of this was commissioned. It is a placeholder for personal projects that do not fit the
through line about systems and people — which is most of what I make when nobody is waiting for
it.
