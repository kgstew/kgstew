When I moved back to Colorado I went looking for a maker space and found a directory that made the
search easy. Then the non-profit hosting it shut down and handed the data to another one, I Am a
Maker.

Together with its founder, Karen Corliss, we brought the directory back — which mostly meant
getting hundreds of listings verified and reachable again rather than writing anything clever.
