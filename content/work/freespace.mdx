freespace began in June 2013 when a building was gifted to the community for a dollar for the
month. The barrier to entry was set deliberately at nothing, and the door stayed open, which is why
the people who turned up had almost nothing in common except wanting to make something.

I was one of the founding members and involved in the day-to-day running of the original space and
of freespace 2.0.
