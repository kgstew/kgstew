Ephemerisle is a yearly gathering in the Stockton river delta where attendees self-organise into
independently governed islands of boats, platforms and improvised floating craft. I led the build
team for the largest of them, Elysium, from 2016 to 2018.

Two weeks on the water at a time, and the problems are all the ones you do not get on land: what
happens to a structure when the tide changes, how a walkway behaves when the thing at each end is
moving independently, how four hundred people get drinking water.

Separately, we built an Alice in Wonderland art boat — a surrealist living room, a floating grass
lawn, a full kitchen, and six camping spots on the roof.
