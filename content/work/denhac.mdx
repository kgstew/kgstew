denhac has been running since 2008 on an all-volunteer team, a member-elected board, and managers
the board appoints. It holds a woodshop, a metalshop, laser cutting, 3D printing, CNC, textiles, an
electronics room and open workshop space.

I was Operations Director from 2022 to 2023, responsible for keeping the tools and equipment
working and for organising members to clean, improve and reorganise the space.

The interesting problem in a place like this is never the tools. It is getting a volunteer
membership to take ownership of improving somewhere they already have a key to.
