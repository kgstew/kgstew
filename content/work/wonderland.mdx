The floating dock platforms we had paid for never materialised — somebody took the money and did
not deliver — which is how Wonderland ended up being built on an old houseboat platform the locals
had been using as a trash barge.

We met the men who ran one of the marinas, they had a hull, and we rebuilt the plan on the spot: an
outrigger second storey improvised onto the platform, with a living room on top of it. A fireplace.
A piano, dragged out into the delta. A floating sod lawn off the back with its own kitchen. Room for
six people to sleep on the roof.

The thing I remember is a cool night with the fire lit, several of us standing around with drinks,
and somebody taking the Alice in Wonderland book off the shelf and starting to read passages aloud.
That was exactly what it had been built for, and it took the boat existing before anyone could have
described it.

Built with Brittany Ferrero.
