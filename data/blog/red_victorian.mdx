# Red Victorian

![Red Victorian](/static/images/blog/red_vic.jpg)

## Experimental boutique hotel and community space

The Red Victorian was an experimental boutique hotel and community space located in San Francisco's Haight-Ashbury district. The building was built in 1904 and the Red Vic has a rich history as a center for counterculture and alternative lifestyles.

I was one of the founding residents of the Red Victorian in 2013, helping to establish it as a hub for community events, art exhibitions and social gatherings.
