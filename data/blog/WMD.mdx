## Manufacturer of Professional Modular Synthesis Instruments

WMD is a manufacturer of music hardware including modular synthesizers and guitar pedals We make tools that inspire creativity through powerful feature sets and well crafted user interfaces.

My work at WMD has focused on embedded software development for our line of Eurorack modular synthesizer modules. These products use microcontrollers to generate and process audio and control voltage signals in real time. I also lead our business team, helping to manage operations, sales and marketing for the company.

As a small business we all wear many hats and I enjoy the variety of work that comes with helping to run a successful hardware company.
